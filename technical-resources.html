Information That Improves Quotation Accuracy

CustomerCompany, contact person, email, phone / WhatsApp and delivery location.
ProductGasket or material description, quantity, thickness and supply form.
Circular gasketOD, ID, PCD, bolt-hole quantity and bolt-hole diameter.
Rectangular / customOverall length, width, internal opening dimensions, hole quantity, hole diameter and pitch.
ApplicationEquipment, fluid / medium, operating temperature and operating pressure where relevant.
AttachmentsDrawing, old-gasket photograph, equipment photograph or specification.

Technical Rule

Do not assume one temperature or pressure rating applies to every sheet or gasket. Exact limits should be checked against the exact grade and service conditions.
