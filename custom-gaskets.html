A Dedicated Custom-Gasket Workflow

Custom gasket jobs are handled separately from wholesale sheet-material orders so dimensional requirements, bolt-hole layouts and application details can be reviewed clearly before cutting.

We Can Review

Technical DrawingsPDF or image-based fabrication drawings.
Existing GasketsPhotographs or physical samples for replacement jobs.
DimensionsOD, ID, PCD, overall length, width, openings and hole details.

Custom Job Process

  1. Send drawing, dimensions, photographs or sample details.
  2. We identify missing dimensions or application information needed for quotation.
  3. Material and geometry are confirmed against the supplied requirement.
  4. Quotation is issued with scope, quantity and delivery details.
Important: We do not display blanket temperature or pressure ratings. Technical limits are stated only where verified for the exact material grade being proposed.
