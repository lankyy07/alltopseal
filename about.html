About ALL TOPSEAL

ALL TOPSEAL is the industrial sealing trading identity of Emma Onyeco Investment Company Limited, based in Lagos, Nigeria.

Industrial Supply With a Technical Sales Mindset

The business supports procurement teams, maintenance engineers, plant managers, workshops and industrial buyers with sealing materials, flange gaskets, packing, rubber, cork and custom gasket requirements.

The website is structured around two distinct buying journeys: wholesale material supply for customers who know the product they need, and custom gasket fabrication for customers working from drawings, dimensions, photographs or physical samples.
